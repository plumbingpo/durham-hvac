Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Greensboro

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that range and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

Minor issues rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a few miserable nights.
