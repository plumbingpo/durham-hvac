Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Durham HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. If a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.
